Shopwalk ACP — Agentic Commerce Protocol Addendum (v1)

This addendum supplements your existing Shopwalk Partner Terms. By enabling the ChatGPT (ACP) channel, you agree to the following in addition to those terms.

1. What ACP enables

Shopwalk publishes your product catalog into the OpenAI Agentic Commerce Protocol feed under Shopwalk's ACP relationship with OpenAI. ChatGPT and other ACP-compliant agents can then surface your products inside the agent experience and, when a payment processor compatible with ACP is configured on your store, complete checkout in-chat. Orders flow back into your WooCommerce store through your existing payment gateway, with no separate payment surface created by Shopwalk.

2. Merchant responsibilities

  • You are responsible for the accuracy of product titles, descriptions, prices, availability, images, and category data published to the ACP feed.
  • You will not list products that violate OpenAI's prohibited-category policy (including but not limited to firearms, controlled substances, adult content, and tobacco).
  • You will honor orders placed through ACP on the same fulfillment, shipping, and returns terms as orders placed through your own checkout.
  • You will respond to moderation flags raised by Shopwalk or OpenAI within a reasonable period.

3. Shopwalk's role

Shopwalk is the contractual party to OpenAI for the ACP feed. Shopwalk may pause, exclude, or remove individual items, the entire feed for your store, or the entire aggregated feed at its discretion when required by OpenAI's policies or by applicable law. Shopwalk will surface the reason for any such action in your WP admin where reasonably possible.

4. Payments and refunds

Payments for ACP-originated orders are processed through your existing payment gateway. Refunds, returns, chargebacks, and disputes are handled via your existing WooCommerce order flow on the same terms as any other order. Shopwalk does not take custody of funds.

5. Pause and uninstall

You may pause the ACP channel from this admin page at any time. Pausing removes your products from the next feed publish and allows any in-flight checkout sessions to complete. Uninstalling the plugin removes your store from the feed at the next publish cycle.

6. Changes to this addendum

Shopwalk may update this addendum from time to time. Material changes will require re-acknowledgement in this admin page before the ACP channel remains enabled.
